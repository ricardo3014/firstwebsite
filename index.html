The concept

Two domains. One room.

Most AI events are built for one audience talking to itself. Puro Matrix is built for the handoff — the moment a business leader's hard-won market knowledge meets an AI leader's technical judgment, and something neither could build alone gets decided. Every session, every day, is designed around one of three questions.

01

What's possible

Frontier thinking from the people setting the pace of AI — where the technology is actually headed, past the hype cycle and the product roadmap.

02

What's available right now

Not vision decks — working systems. Leaders show what's already shipped, already in production, already changing how a real business runs.

03

How to leverage each other

Structured time built for two kinds of expertise to find each other — so the conversation turns into a partnership after the conference ends.

The program

Three days, three questions.

Each day of Puro Matrix answers one of the questions at the center of the conference — moving from vision, to what's real today, to the work of putting people together.

Day 01

What's Possible

Frontier keynotes and moonshot thinking — where AI leaders lay out what's coming before it's conventional wisdom.

  • Opening keynote: the next five years, unfiltered
  • Frontier labs panel — where the ceiling actually is
  • Closed-door roundtable: business leaders ask anything
Day 02

What's Available Now

Live demos and operator case studies — systems already running in production, presented by the people who built and shipped them.

  • Case studies: AI in the P&L, not the pitch deck
  • Live build sessions with working tools
  • "Ask a technical leader anything" clinics
Day 03

How We Leverage Each Other

Structured matchmaking and working sessions — the day conversations turn into partnerships, pilots, and follow-ups.

  • Curated 1:1 matchmaking by domain and need
  • Working sessions: leave with a first draft, not a deck
  • Closing dinner & commitments
